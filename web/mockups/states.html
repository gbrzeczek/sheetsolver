Job state: done · no plan

No plan cuts every part

The search finished without finding a way to cut every part from the panels listed. Common causes:

  • A part is larger than every panel. “Worktop”, 2600 × 600 mm, is longer than every panel (2440 × 1220 mm).
  • Too few panels. Leave a panel’s Quantity empty for “as many as needed”.
  • Settings too strict. “Not allowed” trimming with 2 stages can leave no way to place every part.
